Before we begin

Thank you for taking part in this study. Here's what to expect:

You'll answer a few short questions about yourself, then type a short passage so we can measure your normal typing pattern. After that, you'll watch four short videos, one at a time, and write a few sentences about each one. The whole session takes about 15–20 minutes.

We record what you type and the timing of your keystrokes. Your written responses and how you describe your reaction to each video are used only for this research project.

By continuing, you agree to take part in this study.
